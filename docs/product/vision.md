# Product Vision

Status: Active  
Owner: Founder  
Last Updated: 2026-04-07  
Purpose: Defines what PrepHatch is, who it is for, and how it should be perceived.

## Vision

PrepHatch should become a trusted exam-readiness platform for technical certifications.

## Positioning

PrepHatch should feel like:
- a serious exam simulator
- a guided exam coach
- a trustworthy alternative to low-quality dumps

## What It Is Not

- not a generic course marketplace
- not a broad LMS at launch
- not a random exam dump site
- not an AI chatbot product pretending to be a prep platform

## Core User Promise

Users should feel that PrepHatch helps them:
- pass faster
- feel exam-ready
- identify weak areas clearly
- build confidence before the real exam

## Product Principles

- Trust over flash: explanations, scoring, and access control must feel reliable before the product feels clever.
- Exam realism over content sprawl: one strong package beats many weak packages.
- Clarity over dashboard noise: show learners what to do next, not every metric available.

## Target Users and Pains

Primary users:
- working professionals who need efficient, realistic prep
- career switchers who need stronger explanations and structure
- retake candidates who need fast weak-area identification
- beginners who need confidence-building review loops

Shared pains:
- fragmented resources
- poor explanation quality
- weak readiness signals
- low trust in existing prep products
