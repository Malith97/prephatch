# Voice

- Direct, calm, precise. No hype adjectives (revolutionary, cutting-edge,
  game-changing, "passionate team").
- No invented statistics, customers, testimonials, press mentions, or team
  members. If a claim can't be backed up today, don't make it.
- Describe the product's current state honestly — "in development," never
  "available now."
- Prefer concrete, falsifiable statements over vague ambition
  ("reduces redundant model calls" beats "revolutionizes AI").
- Keep sentences short. Avoid stacked adjectives.
- Treat any drafted copy as a draft for review, not final content.
